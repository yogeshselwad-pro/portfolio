02 / HOW I WORK

Offer first.
Then volume. Then proof.

The order matters. Creative volume against a broken offer just buys the same problem faster, and neither is worth much if the numbers aren’t readable.

01

Offer & unit economics

Work the offer before the bid strategy — checkout design, average order value and contribution margin, plus controlled pricing tests where the economics are the real constraint.

02

Creative volume

Localised creative produced in-house on AI-assisted video workflows, at roughly 40% lower production cost and a brief-to-launch cycle under 48 hours.

03

Measurement

GA4, Meta Pixel and Conversions API with structured A/B testing, so scale decisions run on CAC, contribution margin and customer quality rather than platform-reported ROAS alone.

Having run my own ecommerce business, I read growth past the ad dashboard.

Return rates, checkout design and margin decide whether acquisition spend turns into a business. That is where I start, and it is what I report on.
